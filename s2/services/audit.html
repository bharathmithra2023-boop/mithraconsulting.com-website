Audit & Compliance

End-to-end compliance advisory across 7 global frameworks — from gap analysis to certification readiness. We make compliance achievable and sustainable.

Overview

Compliance Without Compromise

Navigating regulatory requirements is no longer optional — it's a business imperative. Whether you're pursuing your first ISO 27001 certification or maintaining SOC 2 Type 2 compliance, our team brings the frameworks, methodology, and hands-on experience to get you there efficiently.

We don't just issue gap reports. We work alongside your team — drafting policies, designing controls, preparing evidence, and coordinating with auditors — so you can focus on running your business while we handle the compliance journey.

Our coverage spans 7 major compliance frameworks, allowing organisations to manage multiple requirements under one unified programme with less duplication, fewer gaps, and a clear audit trail.

What We Cover

Our Compliance Frameworks

Deep expertise across every major standard — delivered as an integrated programme, not isolated point-in-time assessments.

ISO 27001 — Information Security Management

Comprehensive ISMS implementation aligned with the 2022 revision and its 93 Annex A controls. We guide you from initial gap analysis through to certification body audit.

  • Gap analysis against all ISO 27001:2022 controls
  • Risk assessment & treatment plan development
  • Policy, procedure & control documentation
  • Internal audit programme & management review
  • Certification body selection & coordination
  • Surveillance audit support & continual improvement

ISO 42001 — AI Management System

The world's first AI-specific management standard. We help organisations build responsible, auditable AI governance programmes that meet ISO 42001 requirements.

  • AI risk assessment & impact analysis
  • Responsible AI policy development
  • Algorithmic accountability frameworks
  • AI incident management procedures
  • AI ethics board setup & governance support
  • Conformity assessment readiness review

SOC 2 Type 1 & Type 2

AICPA Trust Service Criteria assessment, evidence collection, and audit readiness for both Type 1 (point-in-time) and Type 2 (12-month monitoring) reports.

  • TSC scoping: Security, Availability, Confidentiality, PI, Privacy
  • Control design gap assessment
  • Evidence collection programme design
  • Type 1 readiness & report preparation
  • 12-month Type 2 monitoring support
  • Auditor liaison & bridge letter preparation

HIPAA — Healthcare Data Protection

Full HIPAA compliance programme covering Administrative, Physical, and Technical safeguards for covered entities and business associates handling PHI.

  • PHI inventory & data flow mapping
  • Administrative safeguards programme
  • Physical & technical safeguards review
  • Risk analysis & risk management plan
  • Workforce training programme
  • Business Associate Agreement review

PCI-DSS v4.0

Cardholder Data Environment scoping, all 12 PCI-DSS v4.0 requirements assessment, and SAQ/QSA support for organisations processing payment card data.

  • CDE scoping & data flow diagramming
  • All 12 requirements gap assessment
  • Network segmentation testing & review
  • SAQ completion guidance (A, B, C, D)
  • Compensating controls documentation
  • QSA liaison & remediation roadmap

GDPR & DPDPA

European GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 compliance — covering data mapping, consent management, DPO advisory, and regulatory readiness.

  • Article 30 data processing register
  • Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA)
  • Privacy notices & consent management
  • Data subject rights procedures
  • DPDPA data fiduciary obligations
  • Cross-border transfer mechanisms (SCCs, BCRs)
