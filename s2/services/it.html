IT Solutions

Fortify your endpoints and communications infrastructure with enterprise-grade security tools — deployed, managed, and monitored by our team.

Overview

Security Where Attacks Begin

Endpoints and email are the two most frequently exploited entry points in modern cyberattacks. Ransomware arrives via a malicious attachment. Business Email Compromise starts with a convincing phishing lure. Credential theft begins with a compromised workstation. Securing these vectors is not optional — it is foundational.

Our IT Solutions practice deploys, configures, and manages enterprise-grade endpoint security and email security platforms — ensuring every device and every inbox in your organisation is protected by tooling that's correctly tuned, actively monitored, and continuously updated against the latest threats.

We work with market-leading vendors including CrowdStrike, SentinelOne, Microsoft Defender, Proofpoint, and Mimecast — selecting the right solution for your environment and operating it with the depth of expertise that in-house teams rarely have bandwidth to develop alone.

What We Deliver

Our IT Security Capabilities

Comprehensive protection for your most-targeted attack surfaces — deployed as a managed service, tuned to your environment, and integrated with your SOC.

Endpoint Security

Enterprise EDR/XDR deployment and management covering every device in your fleet — with behavioural detection, automated response, and zero-trust access enforcement built in from day one.

  • EDR/XDR deployment: CrowdStrike Falcon, SentinelOne, Microsoft Defender for Endpoint
  • Behavioural threat detection and automated response playbooks
  • Application allowlisting and blocklisting with policy management
  • USB and removable media control with granular enforcement policies
  • Full-disk encryption: BitLocker, FileVault, and LUKS deployment and key management
  • Endpoint DLP enforcement for sensitive data classification and egress control
  • OS and third-party patch lifecycle management with SLA tracking
  • Endpoint compliance reporting and fleet health dashboards
  • Proactive threat hunting across the endpoint fleet via SOC integration
  • Zero-trust endpoint access configuration and network access control

Email Security

Multi-layered email security combining Secure Email Gateway protection, advanced sandboxing, DMARC enforcement, and BEC detection — protecting your organisation's most-targeted communication channel.

  • Secure Email Gateway deployment: Proofpoint, Mimecast, Microsoft Defender for O365
  • Advanced phishing and spear-phishing protection with ML-based detection
  • Sandboxing for malicious attachments and URL detonation analysis
  • DMARC, DKIM, and SPF implementation, monitoring, and enforcement
  • Business Email Compromise and executive impersonation detection
  • Email DLP for PII, financial data, and intellectual property egress control
  • Inbound and outbound attachment filtering with file-type policies
  • Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace security hardening and configuration review
  • Email archiving and eDiscovery support for compliance requirements
  • Account takeover detection and anomalous login alerting

How We Deploy & Manage

1

Assessment

Audit your existing endpoint and email security tooling, identify coverage gaps, and map your device fleet and email infrastructure to define deployment scope.

2

Tool Selection

Recommend the optimal platform combination for your environment, size, OS mix, and budget — with vendor-neutral guidance based on your specific threat profile.

Deployment

Monitoring & Optimisation

Why It Matters

Key Benefits

Reduced Attack Surface

EDR and email gateway controls eliminate the most commonly exploited initial access vectors used by ransomware and APT groups.

Phishing Protection

Multi-layer email defences — gateway filtering, sandboxing, DMARC, and BEC detection — stop phishing attacks before they reach the inbox.

Compliance Enablement

Endpoint encryption, DLP, and email archiving satisfy requirements across ISO 27001, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, GDPR, and DPDPA frameworks.

Centralised Visibility

Unified dashboards across your entire endpoint fleet and email environment — with telemetry feeding directly into the SOC for correlation.

Rapid Threat Response

Automated containment — isolate an infected endpoint or quarantine a malicious email with a single action, stopping lateral spread instantly.

Employee Security Awareness

Simulated phishing campaigns and end-user coaching embedded in the email security programme to reduce human-layer risk over time.
