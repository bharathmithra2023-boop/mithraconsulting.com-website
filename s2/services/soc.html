SOC — 24×7 Security Operations

Round-the-clock threat detection, incident response, and threat hunting — your always-on security operations centre.

Overview

Always On. Always Watching.

Threats don't respect business hours — and neither does our Security Operations Centre. Our 24×7 SOC provides continuous monitoring, expert-led threat detection, and rapid incident response across your entire technology environment, from on-premises infrastructure to multi-cloud workloads and SaaS applications.

We deploy and tune industry-leading SIEM platforms — including Microsoft Sentinel, Splunk, and IBM QRadar — aggregating log sources across your network, endpoints, cloud, and identity systems into a single, correlated threat detection engine. Every alert is triaged by experienced analysts using MITRE ATT&CK-mapped detection logic, not just automated rules.

Beyond reactive monitoring, our threat hunting team proactively searches for adversaries already inside your environment — uncovering sophisticated, low-and-slow attacks that evade standard detection, and delivering weekly hunting reports with actionable intelligence.

What We Deliver

Core SOC Capabilities

Four integrated capabilities that together provide comprehensive, continuous protection — from first log ingestion to post-incident improvement.

24×7 Threat Monitoring

Continuous, multi-source security monitoring powered by enterprise SIEM — with MITRE ATT&CK-aligned detection rules and ML-based anomaly detection to catch what rule-based systems miss.

  • SIEM deployment and tuning: Microsoft Sentinel, Splunk, IBM QRadar
  • Multi-source log aggregation: network, endpoint, cloud, and SaaS
  • Real-time correlation rules mapped to MITRE ATT&CK techniques
  • Threat intelligence integration: MISP, STIX/TAXII, commercial feeds
  • ML-based anomaly detection for user and entity behaviour
  • Tiered P1–P4 alerting with defined SLA response times
  • Monthly threat reporting with trend analysis and KPI dashboards

Incident Response

Structured, playbook-driven incident response from initial alert triage through to post-incident reporting — with digital forensics capability and regulatory notification support built in.

  • Rapid triage and severity classification within defined SLA windows
  • Containment and isolation procedures with pre-approved actions
  • Digital forensics and evidence preservation for legal admissibility
  • Root cause analysis and detailed attack timeline reconstruction
  • Eradication, recovery, and full-environment clean-up coordination
  • Post-incident report — executive summary and full technical deep-dive
  • Regulatory notifications: GDPR 72hr, RBI, and CERT-In obligations

Threat Hunting

Proactive, hypothesis-driven hunting campaigns that search for adversaries already operating inside your environment — going beyond what alert-based detection can find.

  • Proactive hypothesis-driven hunting based on current threat intelligence
  • IOC and IOA-based investigation across endpoint, network, and cloud
  • Lateral movement and credential abuse detection campaigns
  • Living-off-the-land (LotL) attack identification and investigation
  • Persistence mechanism discovery across registry, tasks, and services
  • Custom YARA and Sigma rule development from hunt findings
  • Weekly threat hunting reports with findings and detection improvements

Vulnerability Management

Continuous, authenticated vulnerability scanning with threat-intelligence-enriched prioritisation and SLA-tracked remediation — ensuring your highest-risk exposures are always addressed first.

  • Continuous authenticated scanning with Tenable Nessus or Qualys
  • CVSS v3.1 scoring with exploitability context from threat intelligence
  • Patch management coordination with IT and DevOps teams
  • Exception and risk acceptance management with documented approvals
  • SLA-based remediation tracking with escalation workflows
  • Monthly KPI dashboard covering MTTD, MTTR, and remediation rates
Our Process

How We Onboard & Operate

1

Onboarding & SIEM Setup

Connect log sources, deploy collectors, configure SIEM ingestion pipelines, and establish communication and escalation channels with your internal team.

2

Tuning & Baselining

Establish normal behaviour baselines, tune detection rules to your environment, suppress known false positives, and configure alert thresholds for your risk appetite.

3

24×7 Monitoring

Round-the-clock alert triage, investigation, and escalation by our analyst team — with defined SLAs for each severity tier and full shift handover documentation.

4

Detection & Response

Playbook-driven incident response for confirmed threats — from containment and eradication through to recovery, post-incident reporting, and regulatory notification if required.

5

Continuous Improvement

Monthly service reviews, detection rule updates from threat hunting findings, MITRE ATT&CK coverage gap analysis, and ongoing platform optimisation.
