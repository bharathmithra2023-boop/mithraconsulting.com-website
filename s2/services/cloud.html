Cloud Services

Secure, resilient, and compliant cloud infrastructure across AWS, Azure, and GCP — from posture management to disaster recovery.

Overview

Cloud Security You Can Rely On

The cloud unlocks speed and scale — but it also introduces a shared responsibility model that many organisations underestimate. Misconfigurations remain the leading cause of cloud data breaches, and without continuous posture management and a tested recovery strategy, a single misconfigured bucket or miscalculated IAM policy can become a catastrophic incident.

Our cloud services practice covers the three pillars that define cloud security resilience: posture management to ensure your environment is always configured correctly, disaster recovery to guarantee business continuity when the unexpected happens, and backup and restoration to protect your data against ransomware, accidental deletion, and infrastructure failure.

We work across AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform — delivering consistent, framework-aligned security outcomes regardless of your cloud provider mix.

What We Deliver

Our Cloud Service Pillars

Three interconnected capabilities that keep your cloud environments secure, compliant, and always recoverable — across every major platform.

CSPM — Cloud Security Posture Management

Continuous, automated visibility across your multi-cloud estate — detecting misconfigurations, compliance drift, and public exposure before they become incidents.

  • Multi-cloud visibility across AWS, Azure, and GCP in a single pane
  • Automated CIS Benchmark and NIST 800-53 assessments on schedule
  • Misconfiguration detection: open S3 buckets, exposed DBs, IAM over-privilege
  • Public exposure detection with immediate alerting and risk scoring
  • Compliance dashboards for PCI-DSS, HIPAA, and ISO 27001
  • Continuous drift detection with automated remediation approval workflows
  • Real-time alerting integrated with Slack, Teams, and SIEM platforms
  • Monthly cloud security posture reports with trend analysis

Disaster Recovery

Architect, document, and validate a cloud DR strategy that meets your RTO and RPO requirements — tested under real-world conditions, not just on paper.

  • Business impact analysis and RTO/RPO definition for all critical systems
  • DR architecture design: active-passive, active-active, pilot light, warm standby
  • Cloud-native failover automation via Route53, Azure Traffic Manager, Cloud DNS
  • Runbook development with step-by-step recovery procedures
  • Tabletop exercises and live DR failover drills with full reporting
  • Quarterly failover testing with time-stamped RTO/RPO validation evidence
  • Cross-region and cross-cloud DR architecture for maximum resilience
  • Post-DR incident report and lessons-learned recommendations

Backup & Restoration

Implement a 3-2-1-1-0 backup strategy with immutable, ransomware-resistant vaults — and validate every backup with regular, documented restore testing.

  • 3-2-1-1-0 backup strategy design and implementation across all workloads
  • Immutable and ransomware-resistant vaults: AWS Backup, Azure Backup, GCP Backup
  • Cross-region replication with geo-redundant storage tiers
  • Granular retention policies — daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly schedules
  • Restore time validation testing with documented RTO evidence
  • Backup encryption at rest and in transit with key management review
  • Hybrid and air-gapped backup options for critical data sets
  • Backup monitoring and alerting dashboard with failure notifications
Our Process

How We Secure Your Cloud

1

Discovery & Assessment

Inventory your cloud estate, map data flows, identify critical workloads, and assess your current security posture and recovery capability gaps.

2

Architecture Design

Design a target-state cloud security architecture covering posture management, DR topology, and backup strategy aligned to your RTO, RPO, and compliance requirements.

3

Implementation

Deploy CSPM tooling, configure DR automation, implement backup policies, and integrate alerting and dashboards into your existing operations workflow.

4

Testing & Validation

Execute DR failover drills, validate backup restoration times, and run compliance assessments against all configured benchmarks — with full evidence documentation.

5

Continuous Monitoring

Ongoing CSPM monitoring, monthly reporting, quarterly DR testing, and annual architecture reviews to keep pace with cloud evolution and threat changes.
