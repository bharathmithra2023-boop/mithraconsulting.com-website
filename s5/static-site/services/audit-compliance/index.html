Frequently asked

Compliance questions, answered

Which frameworks do you support?

ISO 27001 and ISO 42001, SOC 2, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, GDPR and India's DPDPA. Our practice is led by an ISO 27001 Lead Implementer and supported by CISA-certified auditors.

Do you also issue the certificate?

No, and no consultancy legitimately can. Certification must be issued by an accredited registrar that is independent of your implementation partner. We prepare your evidence, run the internal audit and support you through the certification audit, but the certificate comes from the registrar.

What happens in a gap assessment?

We compare your current controls against every clause and control of the target framework, then return a gap register that states what exists, what is missing, what evidence is required and who owns each remediation item. It becomes the project plan for the rest of the programme.

How does ISO 42001 relate to ISO 27001?

ISO 27001 governs information security management; ISO 42001 governs AI management systems. They share the same management-system structure, so if you already hold ISO 27001 much of the governance, risk and audit machinery is reusable. We onboard AI systems into an existing ISMS rather than building a parallel programme.
