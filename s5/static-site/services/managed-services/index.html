Overview

Your cloud, always secured

Multi-cloud environments are powerful but complex. Security configuration sprawl, identity over-provisioning, and ungoverned resources accumulate silently — creating risk that compounds until it becomes a breach. Most organisations lack the bandwidth or depth to manage cloud security continuously across Azure, AWS, and GCP simultaneously.

Mithra’s Managed Services practice provides end-to-end cloud security operations — handling hardening, monitoring, incident response, compliance alignment, and monthly reporting as a unified, accountable service. We become your cloud security function, not a point-in-time advisory.

Our certified engineers hold platform-specific credentials across all three major clouds, combined with security expertise spanning identity, network, workload, and data protection — bringing the depth that specialised cloud security demands.

Frequently asked

Managed services questions, answered

What is included in managed cloud security?

Continuous monitoring, posture management, identity and access governance, patch and vulnerability management, backup and disaster recovery oversight, and compliance reporting across Azure, AWS and GCP. Scope is agreed per environment during onboarding.

Do you offer private and hybrid cloud hosting?

Yes. We run private cloud hosting and hybrid deployments where workloads must remain on specific infrastructure for regulatory, latency or data-residency reasons, and manage the security of both under the same operating model.

How is a managed engagement priced?

Pricing depends on the size and complexity of your environment, so we scope before quoting rather than publishing a rate card that would not apply to you. A scoping call establishes the estate, coverage hours and reporting you need, and a written proposal follows within two business days.

Can you manage an environment we have already built?

Yes, and most engagements start that way. We begin with an assessment of the existing estate, agree what to remediate before takeover and what to address on an agreed schedule, then move into steady-state operation.
