Overview

Eyes on your environment around the clock

Mithra’s SOC provides enterprise-grade security monitoring without the capital investment of building an in-house operations centre. Our analysts work in rotating shifts to ensure 24-hour coverage, 365 days a year, monitoring your environment for threats using industry-leading SIEM platforms and threat intelligence feeds.

We deploy, tune, and operate SIEM platforms including Microsoft Sentinel, Splunk, and IBM QRadar — integrating log sources across cloud, on-premises, endpoint, and network infrastructure. Our MITRE ATT&CK-aligned detection library covers the full adversary kill chain.

When threats are detected, our analysts triage within 15 minutes for P1 incidents, contain and investigate, and guide your team through remediation with clear, actionable communication at every step.

Frequently asked

SOC questions, answered

Is the SOC genuinely staffed around the clock?

Yes. Coverage runs 24 hours a day, seven days a week, across tier-1 through tier-3 analysts, so escalation does not wait for business hours. Most serious intrusions begin outside them.

What does the SOC actually monitor?

SIEM telemetry from your endpoints, network, cloud platforms and identity providers, correlated and tuned to your environment. Detection logic is aligned to MITRE ATT&CK so coverage can be measured against real adversary techniques rather than alert volume.

What are your response times?

Response targets are set by severity tier and agreed in writing during onboarding, because a suitable target depends on your environment and risk appetite. For general enquiries outside an active incident, we respond within four business hours.

Do you replace our internal security team or work with it?

Either. Some clients have no internal security function and we operate as it; others have a capable team that needs out-of-hours coverage and deeper detection engineering. The operating model is agreed at onboarding.
