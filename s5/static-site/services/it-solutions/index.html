Overview

Security engineering for your endpoints and inbox

Endpoints and email remain the two most exploited entry points for attackers. Mithra’s IT Solutions team deploys, configures, and manages enterprise-grade security tooling to harden these critical attack vectors — protecting your workforce whether they are in the office, remote, or hybrid.

Our engineers are certified across leading security platforms including CrowdStrike, SentinelOne, Microsoft Defender, Proofpoint, and Mimecast. We take a deploy-and-optimise approach: we do not just install software — we tune detection policies, build response playbooks, and monitor alerts.

All implementations are aligned to CIS Controls and NIST CSF to ensure your endpoint and email security investments also contribute to your broader compliance programme.

Frequently asked

IT security questions, answered

What does endpoint security cover?

Endpoint detection and response, device hardening, disk encryption, patch management and application control across laptops, desktops and servers, with policy enforced centrally rather than per machine.

Can you fix our email spoofing problem?

Yes. We implement and monitor SPF, DKIM and DMARC so that only authorised senders can use your domain, then move DMARC to an enforcing policy in stages once legitimate mail flows are verified. This is one of the highest-value, lowest-cost controls available against impersonation.

Do you support Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace?

Both. We harden tenant configuration, conditional access, identity protection and sharing controls, and align the result with the compliance framework you are working to.

What does Zero Trust mean in practice for us?

It means access decisions are made per request based on identity, device posture and context, instead of trusting anything simply because it sits inside the network. In practice that is strong identity, conditional access, least-privilege authorisation and segmentation, rolled out in stages rather than as a single cutover.
