The short version

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and contacts no third party when you load a page. Fonts are served from our own servers. The only external service is the booking calendar, and it loads only if you click to load it. We receive your personal data solely when you choose to send it to us.

Booking a discovery call

Our contact page offers a booking calendar operated by Calendly LLC, a company based in the United States. The calendar does not load automatically. Until you press “Load booking calendar”, no request is made to Calendly and it receives nothing about you.

If you choose to load it, Calendly receives your IP address and may set cookies in your browser, and any details you enter to book a meeting are processed by Calendly under its own privacy notice. Our lawful basis for this is your consent, given by clicking to load it, which you may withdraw at any time by not using the calendar — you can email or telephone us instead.

Server logs

Our web servers record technical information for every request: IP address, timestamp, the page requested, HTTP status, referring page and browser user-agent string. This is standard web server operation and we use it to keep the service available, diagnose faults, and detect and investigate attacks. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in the security and integrity of our systems.

These logs are retained for 90 days and are then deleted automatically.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies of its own. It contains no analytics, advertising, remarketing, session recording or fingerprinting technology, and no social media tracking pixels. Fonts are served from our own infrastructure rather than a third-party font service, so no font provider learns that you visited.

The one exception is the booking calendar described above, which may set cookies once you actively load it. That is why we ask first.

Your rights

Depending on where you are, you may have some or all of the following rights over your personal data.

If the UK or EU GDPR applies to you

  • Access — obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — have inaccurate data corrected
  • Erasure — have your data deleted where there is no continuing basis to hold it
  • Restriction — limit how we use your data while a concern is resolved
  • Objection — object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interests
  • Portability — receive data you gave us in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent — where processing relies on consent, withdraw it at any time

If India’s DPDPA 2023 applies to you

  • Access — a summary of the personal data we process and how
  • Correction and erasure of your personal data
  • Grievance redressal through the Grievance Officer named above
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity

To exercise any of these, email sales@mithraconsulting.com. We will respond within [RESPONSE TIME - GDPR requires one month; DPDPA has its own timeline]. We may need to verify your identity before acting, so that we do not disclose your data to somebody else.

Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority:

  • India — the Data Protection Board of India
  • EU — the supervisory authority in your country of residence or workplace
  • UK — the Information Commissioner’s Office

How we protect your data

This site is served over HTTPS only. It is built as static files with no database, no content management system and no server-side scripting, which removes most of the ways a website is ordinarily attacked. Our servers are firewalled, patched automatically, monitored, and access to them requires named accounts. Access to enquiry data is limited to the people who need it to respond to you.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe you have found a vulnerability in this site, please see the responsible disclosure section of our Terms of Use.

Children

Our services are sold to organisations, and this website is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle personal data we will update this page and revise the date at the top. Where a change is significant we will say so clearly rather than relying on you to notice.
